Part of a textile group that began in 1962.

Hòa Thọ started in 1962 as a weaving mill in Da Nang and grew into one of the largest garment groups in Vietnam, a member of Vinatex. Hoa Tho - Phu Ninh Garment JSC was founded in 2012 with a factory in the Chợ Lò Industrial Cluster, Phú Ninh.

Our focus is trousers. One product, made again and again and made well, is how we keep fit, finish and delivery consistent.

Built around three buyer standards.

The Hoa Tho group exports to the US, the EU, Japan and other markets.

European Union

Buyers ask about chemicals, sustainability and traceability. Share your restricted-substance list and compliance requirements up front, and we plan the order around them.

United States

Large programs, firm delivery windows and retailer audits. We plan lines around your ship dates and keep size sets consistent across repeat orders.

Japan

Buyers inspect closely: straight stitching, clean finishing and careful packing. We sew and check to that standard.

From tech pack to the container.

Six steps, each with a check before the next one starts.

  1. Review the tech pack

    We read your tech pack, spec sheet and size set before we quote.

  2. Pattern and sample

    Patterns are made, a sample is sewn, and it is fitted and approved before bulk.

  3. Fabric and cutting

    Fabric is inspected and cut by marker plan to keep waste low.

  4. Sewing lines

    Operators sew in lines, with checks at key operations: waistband, fly, pockets and hem.

  5. Finishing and inspection

    Pressing, thread trimming, measurement and final inspection.

  6. Packing and shipping

    Packed to your instructions and shipped on schedule to the EU, the US or Japan.
